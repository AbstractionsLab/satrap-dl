# Multi-scenario threat coverage and extensibility

DECIPHER SHALL support automated CTI analysis for at least two distinct threat scenarios
and SHALL allow new threat scenarios to be added without major modification to the core service.

### Rationale
To provide gradual coverage for common threats faced by the target operators and to enable the community to extend DECIPHER for emerging threat types without the need to restructure the service.
